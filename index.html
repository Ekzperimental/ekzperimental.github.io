About

A compact studio for films with memory.

Founded by filmmaker Kostadin Ekzarov, EKZPERIMENTAL is an independent studio based in Bulgaria, working across documentary filmmaking, commercial production, music and the performing arts.

With a background in directing, choreography, visual arts and advertising, Kostadin brings together filmmaking, editing and visual design. Alongside his film work, he is the founder of EKZPERIMENTAL Creative House and co-founder of JAM ON IT Festival and Flava House.

Privacy & Cookies

Draft for local review · Version 0.1 · 23 November 2026. Effective date: to be confirmed before publication.

Who we are

EKZPERIMENTAL EOOD, Bulgaria. Website: ekzperimental.org. For privacy enquiries, contact k.ekzarov@swkz.org.

Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Delivering pages and protecting the service involves technical request data, including IP addresses and browser information. The animation libraries, images and background video are served with the website’s own files.

Email enquiries

If you email us, we receive the contact details, message and any attachments you choose to send so we can respond to your enquiry. Selecting an email link opens your email application; it does not send a message automatically.

Vimeo videos

The Vimeo player loads when you select Play, connecting your browser to Vimeo and sharing technical request information. We use dnt=1 to limit player session data and analytics. This does not prevent all processing or necessary security cookies, and your browser may send Vimeo cookies stored previously. Read Vimeo’s privacy information.

Remembering your language

When you explicitly select Bulgarian or English, we save that choice in your browser’s local storage for up to 30 days. Loading the site does not renew this period. If the setting expires or is unavailable, English is used until you choose a language. You can remove the preference by clearing the website’s stored data. It is not a tracking identifier.

External links

Our social and partner links open other websites with their own privacy practices. These are ordinary links, rather than automatically loaded social media widgets.

Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact k.ekzarov@swkz.org. You may lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.

Before publication

This draft will be finalised after the required controller information, legal bases, retention criteria, international transfer arrangements and consent requirements have been confirmed. A 30-day language setting and Vimeo DNT do not by themselves establish an exemption from consent.

Dance & Theatre

When movement becomes a language.

From the first rehearsal to the stage, we explore the relationship between the body, space and emotion. Working with dancers, choreographers and theatre artists, we bring the performing arts into dialogue with cinema.

Festivals & Events

Beyond the final applause.

From live performances and backstage moments to the energy of the crowd, we capture what makes each event unique. Dynamic recaps, aftermovies and promotional content that keep the experience alive and build anticipation for what's next.

Street Art

From the first sketch to the finished wall.

We create films that capture the creative process behind murals, graffiti and urban art. Through close observation and personal stories, we explore the artists, their work and the spaces they transform.

Music Videos

Every track has its own visual world.

We work with artists, bands and producers to translate music into images. From concept and direction to filming and editing, each video is shaped by the rhythm, character and artistic identity of the music.

Commercial Production

Your brand. A story worth sharing.

We create commercial films and branded content that combine cinematic imagery with thoughtful storytelling. From concept to final delivery, we focus on the identity, message and audience of each project.

Documentary Films

Real people. Stories worth telling.

We create documentary films and portraits that explore people, culture and the world around us. From initial research to the final edit, our approach is rooted in authenticity and a strong cinematic vision.

Video Editing & Post-Production

Where the story takes shape.

Editing is where structure, rhythm and emotion come together. We collaborate remotely with filmmakers, production companies and agencies, providing creative editing, colour correction, sound finishing and final delivery across a range of formats.
